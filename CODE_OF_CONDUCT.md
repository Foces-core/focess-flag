# Code of Conduct

The FOCES Flag community welcomes participation from people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience. We are committed to making project spaces
respectful, constructive, and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and code on their merits; accept that people may disagree.
- Give constructive feedback and be open to feedback about your own work.
- Respect other people's privacy and do not share private information without
  permission.
- Help keep discussions focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, demeaning, or deliberately disruptive conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or other conduct that makes project spaces unsafe.
- Knowingly sharing malicious content, credentials, or harmful code.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, code
reviews, and project-related communications. Repository maintainers may remove
or edit content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to
address harmful behavior. Maintainers will consider the circumstances and aim
to respond fairly and proportionately.

To report a conduct concern, contact a repository code owner privately through
GitHub. If your concern involves one code owner, contact another listed owner
where possible. Do not use the public issue tracker for sensitive reports.

This policy is intended to support a welcoming community, not to restrict
good-faith technical disagreement.
